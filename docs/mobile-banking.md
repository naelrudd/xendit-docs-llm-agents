# SCB-Mobile Banking

![Logo of SCB featuring a stylized spade design and the letters SCB.](https://cdn.document360.io/217abc43-8677-41fb-a81d-fceeb1fa0358/Images/Documentation/SCB_EASY.jpg)

Siam Commercial bank (SCB) mobile banking is one of Thailand’s most widely used banking services for everyday needs.

---

## Payment flow

[Video](https://cdn.document360.io/217abc43-8677-41fb-a81d-fceeb1fa0358/Images/Documentation/SCB%20Mobile%20Banking%20New.mp4)

1. On checkout page, end customer selects SCB Mobile Banking
2. Redirect end customer to SCB Mobile Banking app
3. End customers confirm the amount
4. End customer click confirm the payment
