# Krungthai Bank Mobile Banking

![](https://cdn.document360.io/217abc43-8677-41fb-a81d-fceeb1fa0358/Images/Documentation/Krungthai_NEXT.jpg)

Krungthai Bank (KTB) is a leading Thai financial institution known for its strong digital banking services and role in handling government transactions. It provides seamless banking solutions for individuals and businesses nationwide.
