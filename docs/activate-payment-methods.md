# Activate payment methods

Find out which payment methods you can use based on your business entity type in the table below. You can use filter or search to find information about specific payment method.

[Embedded content](https://airtable.com/embed/appYc7UnQq5CmKe0G/shrlV9Nv97iK3QLdx)

### Activation methods (applicable after [account verification](https://xendit-docs.document360.io/docs/create-account#how-to-verify-your-business))

- Instant - Available immediately after your account has been verified
- Dashboard - Available by request on Xendit dashboard
- Contact CS - Available by request to help@xendit.co

## **How to activate payment methods on Xendit dashboard**

1. Log in to your [Xendit dashboard](https://dashboard.xendit.co/)
2. Click **Payment Channels** on your sidebar
3. Click **Activate Payment Channels**
4. Select the payment method you’d like to activate
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the process

> [!NOTE]
> Note:
> 
> - Some payment methods may require additional documentation or verification
>   - **Kredivo** requires an additional User Acceptance Testing (UAT) process after you submit an activation request. You’ll need to complete this UAT to accept Kredivo transactions. Please refer to [Kredivo UAT](/v1/docs/kredivo-uat) for detailed steps.
> - Some payment methods might not be available for certain industries
